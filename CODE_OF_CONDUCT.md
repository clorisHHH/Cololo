# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

COLOLO contributors are expected to be respectful, constructive, and inclusive. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the repository owner privately through GitHub. Please include enough context to investigate and avoid sharing private data publicly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and productive.
